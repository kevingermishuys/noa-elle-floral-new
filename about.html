About

Floral art, not a supermarket bouquet.

Nòa-Elle Floral Studio is Inge Culbert's studio, working out of Pretoria — full wedding and event floral design alongside hand-wrapped arrangements for the everyday reasons that matter.

The studio

Every arrangement is made once.

All Nòa-Elle floral arrangements are one of a kind, elegantly wrapped in signature Nòa-Elle styling with a discreet water source included. This is not your ordinary supermarket bouquet — it's floral art.

There is no standing recipe and no set of pre-built bouquets waiting in a fridge. Each arrangement is composed by hand from what the season is actually giving — king protea, garden roses, dahlias, lisianthus, olive and eucalyptus foliage — so no two leave the studio the same.

That's also why the gallery names its stems. Tap or hover any photograph and it tells you exactly what went into it.

What you get

The part other florists leave out.

Signature wrapping

Hand-wrapped in the Nòa-Elle style, with a discreet water source built in so it arrives — and stays — fresh.

Seasonal, and local

Built around what's genuinely in season, leaning on South African blooms — the king protea is a house favourite for good reason.

Ordered like a conversation

No checkout, no forms. Message Inge on WhatsApp, say what the occasion is, and she builds it to taste. Payment by EFT.

Weddings & events

Built around your date, not a package.

Wedding and event work runs from a single consultation on WhatsApp — date, venue, guest count, palette. From there it's designed to your vision: white-and-green tablescapes, warm dried and earthy palettes, arches, garlands, bud vases and everything between.

The gallery is the portfolio. It is all her own work, and there is a lot of it.
